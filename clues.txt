**Evidence – Case 001: The 9:17 Incident**


**Clue 1 – The Camera Gap**


**Source: security system report**

**Time: 9:16 – 9:19PM**

The gallery’s main camera stopped recording for exactly three minutes. No system failure was reported.

What it suggests: Someone may have deliberately interrupted the recording


**Clue 2 – The Access Log**


**Source: Electronic maintenance door**

**Time: 9:17PM**

David Reed’s access card unlocked the maintenance corridor.

What it suggests: Someone used David’s card, or David used it himself.


**Clue 3 – The Insurance Email**


**Source: Email from the gallery’s owner**

**Time: Two days before the theft**

Elena Cross asked her insurer whether the painting’s disappearance would be covered at its newly increased valuation.

What it suggests: Elena may have had a financial motive.


**Clue 4 – The Collector’s Offer**


**Source: Email from Sophie Lane**

**Time: 8:32 PM**

Sophie offered Elena an unusually large sum for The Blue Room. Elena refused.

What it suggests: Sophie wanted the painting badly enough to consider breaking the law.


**Clue 5 – The Guard’s Complaint**


**Source: Email from Marcus Vale**

**Time: 8:51 PM**

**Marcus complained that the camera had been unreliable all week and that he had reported the problem to management.**

**What it suggests: The camera gap might not be deliberate.**

**Clue 6 – The Delivery Receipt**

**Source: Gallery loading-dock receipt**

**Time: 9:24PM**

**A maintenance supply delivery was marked as collected, but the driver wrote that nobody signed for it.**

**What it suggests: The delivery record may have been used to disguise movement through the loading-dock.**






**Clue 7 – The Contradiction**


**Source: David’s email to Marcus**

**Time: 9:41PM**

**David wrote: “I handed my access card back before I left at 8:45”**

**But the access log shows his card was used at 9:17PM**

**What it suggests: David’s claim is incomplete, or someone else had his card.**


**Clue 8 – The Missing Attachment**


**Source: Recovered email draft**

**Time: 9:18 PM**

**A draft email contains the words: “It’s done. The frame is still there.”**

**The attachment that was supposed to accompany the message is missing.**

**What it suggests: The painting may have been removed from its frame, and someone tried to communicate about the theft.**
