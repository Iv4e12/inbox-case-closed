**CASE 001 – SOLUTION**


**THE 9:17 INCIDENT**

**The culprit is… Drum-roll please……….…..**


**David Reed – The maintenance worker!**


**Motive: David discovered that the painting had a hidden original sketch underneath its backing. He planned to sell the sketch seperately to a private collector**


**How he did it: David kept his access card after claiming he returned it. At 9:17 PM, he used it to enter the maintenance corridor while the camera was temporarily offline.**

**He removed the painting from its frame, concealed the canvas inside a maintenance-material container and used the loading dock to get it out.**


**What happened at 9:17PM?**

**David used his access card to enter the maintenance corridor. The camera gap concealed his movements, but the access log recorder the card’s use.**


**Why the other suspects look guilty**

**Marcus Vale – The camera gap makes him suspicious, but he’d reported camera problems before the theft.**

**Elena Cross: The insurance email suggests a financial motive, bbut it does not prove she stole the painting.**

**Sophie Lane: She wanted the painting, but her offer was refused.**


**The decisive evidence:**

** David’s card was used at 9:17PM**

**David claimed he returned the card before “leaving” at 8:45 PM**

**The recovered draft says “It’s done. The frame is still there.”**

**The unsigned delivery receipt suggests the loading dock was used without a proper handover.**


***Final answers:***

***Who: David Reed***

***How: He used the maintennace corridor and loading dock.***

***Why: To steal the hidden original sketch for private sale.***

***What happened at 9:17PM: He used his access card to enter the corridor***
