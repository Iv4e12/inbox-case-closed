THE 9:17 INCIDENT

A priceless painting called **The Blue Room** disappeared from a gallery at 9:17PM


Here’s the twist:

The gallery’s security system show that nobody entered or left. Your job is to figure out:


**1. Who stole the painting?**

**2. How did they get it out?**

**3. Why did they steal it?**

**4. What happened at exactly 9:17PM?**


 You’ll discover the answers by piecing together emails, timestamps, receipts, attachments and contradictions.
